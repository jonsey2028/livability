Layer 4 · Organization & Leadership

The multidisciplinary team the concept requires.

The framework calls for a veteran CEO candidate and a capable CEO with meaningful authority to build an executive team across seven functions — each with a named near-term deliverable.

Functions & Deliverables

Seven functions, each with a named near-term deliverable.

The framework recommends identifying a veteran CEO candidate and an experienced CEO with real authority to build their own team — governance and decision rights are the first deliverable of the program, not an afterthought.
Government & Policy Interface

Complete the regulatory fact base before requesting policy intervention.

The framework is explicit: senior-level outreach without demonstrated capital, credible economics and a validated regulatory pathway would weaken the proposal. Engagement is sequenced — agencies and decision authorities are mapped for animal health, importation, veteran participation, agricultural finance and national-security framing — and requests are separated into what existing authority covers versus what requires a new rule, waiver, appropriation or legislation.
