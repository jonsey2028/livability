Evidence Before Scale

A stage-gated validation program — nine working assumptions, eight workstreams, six stages.

Livability advances through evidence-based validation gates rather than directly into external promotion. Each workstream must clear its gate before the program advances. Nothing external is claimed until it is proven.

Eight Workstreams

Where the validation work actually happens.

A · Market & Herd Need

Confirm U.S. breeding herd size/trend, beef supply outlook, price dynamics, domestic heifer availability, and the scale of any genuine supply gap.

B · Animal Health & Import Regulation

Confirm regional eligibility, disease status, import rules, quarantine/testing/vaccination requirements and realistic approval pathways.

C · Agricultural Operations

Model acquisition, transportation, feedlot/quarantine, breeding, rancher leasing, mortality, calf yield, grazing and feed requirements.

D · Veteran Ownership & Economics

Define ownership, lease economics, distributions, asset growth, protections, suitability and exit rights — from the veteran’s perspective.

E · Finance & Underwriting

Define entity structure, capital stack, investor returns, collateral, insurance, liquidity, accounting, tax and downside scenarios.

F · Legal & Compliance

Assess contracts, securities implications, animal ownership/custody, insurance, veteran protections, REIT concepts and smart-contract enforceability.

G · Government Strategy

Identify required authorities, agencies, policy asks, and the evidence package needed before engagement.

H · Pilot Design

Design a bounded proof-of-concept validating cattle economics, lease mechanics, veteran participation and operational controls before national scaling.

Discipline

Honesty as a program feature.

What this means in practice

The framework forbids presenting discussion estimates externally until documented. It forbids senior-level government outreach before the operating and financial model survives validation. It forbids scale claims before pilot data. Institutional capital expects this discipline; the program is built around it.
