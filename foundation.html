An Evidence-Based Operating System

Veenhoven’s four qualities of life drive every program and every metric.

Every cohort is measured against this framework — before and after — the same methodology behind the World Database of Happiness. Standardized, comparable impact data, not anecdotes.

Livability of Environment

Therapeutic farm campuses, calm and accessible design, and community infrastructure that fits veterans’ needs for safety, structure and purpose.

Life-Ability of the Person

Hands-on skills training, clinically supervised trauma support delivered alongside the work, and peer networks that rebuild trust and capability.

Utility of Life

Growing food for community, mentoring fellow veterans, supplying food banks and markets — a new mission after military service.

Appreciation of Life

The measured outcome: life satisfaction tracked with standardized scales, building a veteran-specific evidence base the whole field can use.

Three Layers, One Platform

Each layer proves the next. Each layer is separately fundable.

1

Veteran Agritherapy & Enterprise Hubs

25–40 acre working farm campuses serving cohorts of 20–30 veterans: agricultural training, on-site clinical support, and enterprise incubation. Partners include VA Vocational Rehab, USDA AgVets, and the Farmer Veteran Coalition network.

Phase 1 · $1.5–3M · Philanthropy & federal grants
2

Community Land Trust

Land held in perpetuity by the trust. Residents hold 99-year ground leases and pay one Community User Fee — replacing rent, mortgage, property tax, insurance and HOA charges — indexed to wages, not land speculation.

Phase 2 · $10–25M · Impact & corporate capital
3

National Community Livability

The “15-Minute Veteran Community” — a U.S. pilot community of 500–1,000 residents with clinic, wellness center and commercial core — scaling region by region in evidence-gated tranches.

Phase 3 · $100M+ · Institutional & corporate anchors

Not real estate speculation — housing as a service

Residents never carry mortgages, property taxes or maintenance risk. One consolidated fee, indexed to wages. When housing burden falls from 35–50% of income to 15–20%, the freed-up income recirculates through the local economy — to the same stakeholders whose patient capital funds the trust. That loop, not speculation, is the return.
