Layer 3 · Veteran Model

Ownership with protections built in from day one.

The veteran proposition is a distinct program architecture — not a secondary benefit of the cattle program. Veterans own productive assets; ranchers operate them; each cycle compounds the veteran’s ownership.

The Veteran’s Role

  • Owner/investor in productive breeding cattle
  • No day-to-day ranch operation required
  • A contractual share of offspring each breeding cycle
  • Asset growth across repeated cycles
  • Defined exit rights and liquidity provisions

The Rancher’s Role

  • Custody and daily husbandry under a defined lease
  • Breeding management and veterinary coordination
  • Operational management on established grazing and feedlot capacity
  • Performance responsibilities spelled out in the lease terms
Growth Objective

What a veteran’s holding looks like over repeated cycles.

Each breeding cycle returns offspring to the veteran. Returned offspring join the veteran’s herd and produce in the next cycle — compounding ownership through biology, not paper. The framework places economic modeling of this compounding in workstream D, from the veteran’s perspective.

Cycle 1

Veteran’s initial leased cattle produce; the agreed share returns to the veteran.

Cycle 2

Returned offspring join the herd; the veteran’s producing base is larger.

Cycle N

The veteran’s productive herd has grown through repeated biological compounding.

Protection Objective

Safeguards designed before any veteran is enrolled.

Financial protections

  • Participant suitability standards and full disclosures
  • Liquidity provisions and defined exit rights
  • Mortality and disease risk allocation
  • Insurance requirements on leased cattle
  • Default, recovery and exit mechanics

Operational protections

  • Custody controls and defined valuation methods
  • Audit rights on herd records
  • Standardized lease terms with performance duties
  • Protection from unsuitable financial exposure
Enrollment is not open. Participation, if and when offered, would occur through formal offering documents defining ownership, risk and exit terms — after validation clears.
